## Required characteristics

- Use a continuous world journey instead of a page-sized outer frame.
- Keep the deep navy JRPG App Shell and HUD stable between routes.
- Connect reading, subtopic quiz, review, and chapter final quiz as visible journey nodes.
- Recompose mobile layouts instead of scaling down desktop layouts.
- Use natural terrain, paths, bridges, mist, stairs, and lighting as scene separators.
- Add route and scene transitions without moving or resizing the HUD.
- Remove cream page backgrounds; parchment is limited to small in-world objects.
- Every route-level design must be generated at desktop and 393px for owner approval before implementation.
